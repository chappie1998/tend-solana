The problem 02 / 08

A directional view should not require opaque risk.

Leveraged perpetuals

Margin and liquidation mechanics can turn a simple view into an open-ended decision process.

Traditional options

Strikes, expiries, spreads, and payoff curves make the true outcome harder to compare at a glance.

People need a clear maximum loss and a payoff they can understand before committing capital.

Why Solana 04 / 08

Trust the position state, not a database balance.

A signed quote becomes a program-owned position with reserved writer collateral. The wallet signs the transaction; a portfolio entry appears only after the fill is confirmed on-chain.

Removing the chain would remove verifiable collateral reservation and independent position settlement.

Business model 06 / 08

Revenue aligned with settled wins.

The planned model is a disclosed protocol fee on winning payouts. Losing positions have no payout fee. Today Tend is pre-launch, with no reported customers or revenue.

A real-money launch requires security audits, liquidity, market-data rights, and jurisdiction-specific legal review.
